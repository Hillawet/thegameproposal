Our Story

Who We Are

Over 20 years of passion for the sea and accommodation, committed to delivering extraordinary experiences on the water for every guest.

Our Heritage

Born From a Love of the Sea

The GAME BOAT RENTALS COMPANY is located in Caribbean Bay, Kariba in Zimbabwe. This Boat Rental was opened in 2002 by Mr Thando Mhlathuzi. The Game Boat Rentals is one of the biggest boat rentals in Kariba and For Accommodation. There are different sections of renting boats. The first Section is called Luxury Boats Section, this is for renting luxury boats. In this section there are four employees and per week about ninety (90) customers are attended. The second section is called Fishing Boats Section, this is for renting fishing boats. In Fishing Boats Section, per week above two hundred (200) customers rent fishing boats.. What began as two modest vessels at Harbor Bay Marina has grown into one of the region's most respected and beloved charter operations.

Our name comes from our roots in big game fishing — a tradition that still beats at the heart of everything we do. Today, alongside our world-class fishing charters, we offer an expansive range of luxury cruises, private events, corporate charters, and coastal adventures.

Every boat we operate, every crew member we hire, and every charter we run reflects our founding belief: that time on the water should be extraordinary.
